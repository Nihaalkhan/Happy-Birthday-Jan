A few words from me

Dear Mahira,

Happy Birthday, Mahira.

There was a time when you were an important part of my life, and I believe you knew just how much you mattered to me. Some people leave a mark that time doesn't simply erase.

Even though life moves forward and takes people in different directions, I didn't want your special day to pass without wishing you something beautiful.

I hope this new chapter of your life brings you peace when things get difficult, laughter on ordinary days, success in everything you work for, and people who genuinely make you feel appreciated.

May every silent prayer find its way to an answer. May your heart always have reasons to smile. And may life surprise you with more happiness than you ever expected.

No expectations, no hidden requests. Just a sincere wish for someone who once meant a lot.

Janam din bohat bohat mubarak ho, Mahira. ♡

With warm wishes,
Someone who wishes you well.
